Hello, I am **Yongjung Kim**, an observational astronomer interested in quasars across cosmic time. I am currently an **Assistant Professor** at [**Sejong University**](https://www.sejong.ac.kr) in Seoul, Republic of Korea. 

Before joining Sejong University, I worked at the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute ([KASI](https://www.kasi.re.kr)), Kyungpook National University ([KNU](https://www.knu.ac.kr)), and the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics ([KIAA](http://kiaa.pku.edu.cn)).
I earned my Ph.D. in Astronomy from Seoul National University ([SNU](https://www.snu.ac.kr/)), under the guidance of Professor Myungshin Im.
You can find more details about my academic journey on my [CV](https://yongjungkim.github.io/cv/) page.

I am leading the **SQuARE** Group at Sejong University, dedicated to exploring the nature and evolution of quasars and galaxies across cosmic time.
Our work combines large multi-wavelength survey data and statistical methods to provide essential insights into quasar evolution, black hole growth, and the connection between quasars and their host galaxies. Please visit the [SQuARE Group](https://yongjungkim.github.io/square) and [Research](https://yongjungkim.github.io/research) pages for more details.
